Age-based FD eligibility ​

Some Sri Lankan banks publish separate fixed-deposit products or rates for senior customers and minors. These are product eligibility rules, not universal age rules: each bank defines its own cutoff, boundary wording, minimum deposit, permitted tenure and account-opening requirements.

How FD Cal represents age eligibility ​

The bank product CSVs can record:

FieldMeaning
customer_segmentgeneral, senior or minor
min_age, max_agePublished lower and upper age bounds; blank means the bank did not publish that bound in the source we verified
min_age_inclusive, max_age_inclusiveWhether the corresponding boundary is inclusive
guardian_requiredWhether the source explicitly requires a parent or guardian

The browser exposes Include senior citizen FDs and Include child / minor FDs switches. They default off, preserve all general products, and add only the selected age-segment products. The switches do not collect a date of birth or claim that a particular person qualifies. For products actually booked by a simulation, the standalone report displays the age bounds, inclusive/exclusive boundary and guardian requirement recorded in the catalogue. These conditions remain informational and must be confirmed with the bank. A bank's wording such as “over 60” is stored as a strict lower bound, not silently rounded to “60+”.

Identified products not yet added as simulated rate rows ​

  • People's Bank senior / Parinatha FDs: the term-deposit page lists a senior FD for customers over 55 (minimum LKR 100,000; 12/24 months) and a special senior product for customers over 60 (minimum LKR 5,000; cumulative cap LKR 1.5 million; interest exempt from income tax). The rate page's Parinatha bands show a LKR 5,000 minimum, so the rate/product mapping is unresolved. The over-60 cap and tax treatment are also not enforced by the simulator. These rates remain in the research ledger, not the optimizer. See term-deposit terms and rate schedule.
  • People's Bank Minor FD: the bank says the product is for children up to 18 years, can be opened by a parent or guardian, and pays 0.5 percentage points above normal FD rates. The product page does not clearly publish a separate minimum deposit or complete product-specific rate/tenure table. We are not copying the adult minimum or deriving simulated rates until the minor product's terms are confirmed.
  • RDB Senior Citizen FD: the official schedule states over 60 and publishes one-year maturity and monthly rates, but does not state a senior-product minimum. The general FD minimum is not borrowed; the senior option remains in the research ledger only.
  • Nations Trust Bank — Nations Prabuddha Senior Citizen FD: the official key facts document confirms an over-60 product with 12–60 month tenures and monthly or maturity payout. Current senior-specific rates are not yet confirmed.
  • Sampath Bank — Sanhinda senior special FD: the official declaration confirms resident Sri Lankan citizens over 60 and a scheme-wide LKR 1.5 million deposit cap across banks. Current product rates and full terms are not yet confirmed.
  • HDFC Vishrama Udana and SMIB Senior Citizen FD: official product pages state an over-60 eligibility rule. Their senior product rates are not added here until a current, matching rate schedule is verified.

Research ledger ​

The machine-readable research ledger includes products whose eligibility is known even when a current matching rate or minimum deposit is not yet safe to simulate: age_eligibility.csv. Its research_status distinguishes catalogue-ready entries from incomplete or conflicting evidence.

Data policy ​

No age threshold is inferred from a product name, tax treatment, general account eligibility or another bank's rules. No dummy rates are inserted. If a bank publishes a rate but omits an age cutoff, or publishes eligibility but not a current rate, that information remains documented as incomplete rather than becoming an optimisable product.

Age eligibility is evaluated at the product/account-opening point where the bank's terms say so. Where the bank does not specify the exact reference date or boundary, the catalogue must retain that uncertainty rather than invent a rule.
